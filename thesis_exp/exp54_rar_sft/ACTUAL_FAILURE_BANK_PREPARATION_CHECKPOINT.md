## ODPO formula check

The original ODPO objective defines the estimated reward as the beta-scaled
policy/reference log ratio and subtracts the preference offset outside that
beta scaling. Therefore, when `Delta` denotes the unscaled
chosen-minus-rejected log-ratio difference, the direct form is:

```text
-log sigmoid(beta * Delta - offset)
```

It is not `-log sigmoid(beta * (Delta - offset))` unless the inside quantity is
defined as `offset / beta`.

Source: Amini, Vieira, and Cotterell, *Direct Preference Optimization with an
Offset*, Findings of ACL 2024, Eq. 7c and Eq. 12:
https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.592/
